About F.F Manufacturer

We make leather belts for men and women in Jajmau, Kanpur, the heart of India's leather trade. Our range covers formal dress belts, textured and stitched belts, casual vintage-finish belts, braided belts, and women's python-print, stretch and colour belts.

We sell directly to individual buyers and in bulk to retailers, uniform suppliers and corporate gifting buyers. Every order ships with a GST invoice.
